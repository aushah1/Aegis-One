Hi, I'm Aegis.

I'll show you which companies watch you on each site, grade the site's privacy from A to F, and offer to decline the extra cookies for you. Here's exactly what I keep and where it goes.

What I keep, on this computer only

  • Site names you visit (like cnn.com, never full web addresses), the tracking companies each one contacted, and the names of the cookies it set (never their contents), your cookie choice there and short notes from its privacy policy. Deleted 7 days after your last visit.
  • Daily totals, numbers only with no site names, for the Aegis's impact and Safety views. Deleted after 90 days.
  • Your settings, and my AI brain if you download it. That's all.

I never read what you type, never store passwords or cookie contents, and never send your browsing to anyone: not to the Aegis One team, not to an AI company, not to advertisers.

What I do on websites

  • When a site shows a cookie banner, I offer to click Reject for you, then check the site saved your choice. Turn on Protect me automatically and I'll decline without asking.
  • Where a site shows a "Do not sell or share my personal information" button, I click it once to opt you out.
  • I warn you about banners with no Reject button and about companies on U.S. government lists.

Turn each of my pop-ups on or off in Settings › Aegis on websites.

The only things I download

  • Public lists of data breaches (Have I Been Pwned) and data brokers (California), once a day. They're the same files for everyone, so they reveal nothing about you.
  • My AI brain, Gemma 4 (2 GB), only if you click Download Aegis's brain in Ask Aegis. It runs inside Chrome on your computer. (If you use Ollama instead, I talk to the Ollama app on this computer and download nothing.)
  • A site's own privacy policy, from that same site and without your cookies, then not again for a week, so I can tell you whether it says it sells your data. You can turn this off in Settings.
